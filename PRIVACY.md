# Privacy Policy — Canvas

Effective 26 August 2026.

Canvas is a notes app published by **ElecTream** (Leeam Sweeney).

## What we collect

- **Notes on device.** Titles, content, and attached images are stored locally (SQLite).
- **Google account (if you sign in).** Google Sign-In may receive your user id, email, and display name. Leftover Firebase Auth/Firestore may sync or import notes if those paths are enabled.
- **Camera and photos.** Only when you add an image to a note.

We do not sell this data. We do not use it for ads.

## Why we need it

Notes and optional images are the product. Sign-in is only if you choose Google.

## Retention and deletion

Uninstalling removes the on-device database. Delete images you attached by removing them from the note. If a Google/Firebase account is linked, request deletion via the Play listing developer contact until in-app account delete ships.

## Permissions (Android)

Camera, read media images. Internet may be merged in by plugins when sign-in or fonts load.
